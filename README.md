## Research

- *Hybrid Transformer-Diffusion Model for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting*, published by
  IEEE. A Transformer models the overall seasonality and a diffusion network refines the local
  detail. Parallel sampling with a fast DPM solver cut the denoising steps by up to 80% without
  hurting forecast quality.
- *Community Detection in Financial Networks*, published in a Scopus-indexed journal. A
  dual-graph GNN that finds suspicious clusters in transaction networks to support
  anti-money-laundering investigations. I led a team of four.
